Rocket Fuel Management: How to Never Run Dry

In Cyber Rocket Roadster the fuel bar is the real health bar. You can dodge perfectly and still lose a run if the tank is empty when a tall barrier arrives. Here is how fuel works and how to manage it.

The numbers

A full tank gives roughly four seconds of continuous thrust. While the wheels are on the road, fuel refills at about two thirds of that burn rate, so a completely empty tank needs about six seconds on the ground to fill again. A green fuel cell restores more than a third of the tank instantly.

SituationWhat happens to fuel
Holding the boosterDrains fast
Wheels on the roadRefills slowly
In the air, booster offNo change
Collecting a fuel cellLarge instant refill

Burst and glide

The most common mistake is flying high and holding the button for safety. It works for a few seconds and then you drop with an empty tank. Use short bursts instead. Tap to climb, release to glide, tap again to correct. In the air with the booster off you use no fuel at all, so every moment spent gliding is free.

Use the road as a charging pad

Whenever the road ahead is clear, land. Even two seconds on the ground returns a noticeable slice of the tank. Think of the road as a charging pad that you visit between hazards, not a danger to avoid.

Plan fuel around fuel cells

Green cells appear alone and at a random height. If you see one coming, you can spend more fuel beforehand because you will refill soon. If you see none, save fuel for the next hazard.

Quick rules

  • Keep the bar above one quarter. When it turns red, land and recharge.
  • Never hold the booster just because the sky looks safe.
  • Spend fuel on hazards, not on coins you can reach by gliding.
