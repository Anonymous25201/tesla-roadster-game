Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What this site is

Cyber Rocket Roadster is a free browser game. It is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesla, Inc.

Data the game stores

Your best score is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. We do not run accounts and we do not collect names, email addresses or other personal details through the game. If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners are third-party vendors that use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or at aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads are shown according to your consent choices.

Hosting and service worker

The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type for security and performance. The site also saves its own pages in your browser cache through a service worker so the game can load offline.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can clear your saved best score and cached files at any time by clearing your browser's site data. You can also block cookies in your browser settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
