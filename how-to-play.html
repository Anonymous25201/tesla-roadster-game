How to play Cyber Rocket Roadster

One control, a rocket booster and a lot of things that want to hit you. This page explains everything the game does so you can get a higher score faster.

Controls

DeviceTo fire the rocketsTo fall
Phone or tabletTouch and hold anywhere on the game screenLift your finger
Desktop with a mousePress and hold the left mouse button on the gameRelease the button
KeyboardHold Space, Up Arrow or WRelease the key

The same key or tap also starts a run from the title screen and restarts after a crash. After a crash there is a short pause of about half a second before a restart is accepted, so you do not skip the result screen by accident.

The goal

Your truck drives forward on its own. You only decide how high it is. Stay alive as long as you can, collect coins and fuel on the way, and beat your best score. The game gets faster as your score climbs, so the longer you survive, the less time you have to react.

Rocket fuel

The bar at the top right is your rocket fuel. Holding the booster uses it up quickly, in a little over four seconds from a full tank. When the tank is empty, the rockets cut out until you refuel. Fuel comes back slowly while the wheels are on the ground, and green fuel cells in the air refill a large part of the tank at once. The bar turns red when you are running low.

The key habit is to use the rockets in short bursts rather than holding them down. A burst lifts you over a hazard, and letting go lets gravity bring you back to the road, where the tank slowly refills.

What you will meet

Barriers

Grey blocks with orange warning stripes standing on the road. They come in different heights. Short ones can be hopped with a quick tap, tall ones need a real burst.

Drones

Small hovering drones with a red light. They float at different heights and bob up and down, so aim for a gap in the middle of their movement instead of the height where you first saw them.

Meteors

Burning rocks that fall from the top of the screen at an angle. They come with a bright trail that shows the direction they are moving. Fly under them or hold position until they have passed.

What you can collect

  • Gold coins appear in arcs of five. Each coin adds ten points to your score, so a full arc is worth fifty.
  • Green fuel cells are rarer and appear alone. Each one gives you back a large part of your fuel.

Scoring

Your score has two parts: the distance you have travelled and the coins you have collected. Distance counts up continuously while you survive, and coins add a bonus on top. Your best score is saved in your browser on your own device, so it will still be there next time unless you clear your browser data. See the privacy policy for details.

Playing on a phone

The game works in portrait and landscape. Landscape gives you more room to see hazards coming, so if you are struggling on a narrow screen, turn the phone sideways. Closing the browser tab or switching apps pauses your finger input, so you will not be flying when you come back.
